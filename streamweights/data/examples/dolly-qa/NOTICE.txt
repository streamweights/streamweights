Passages are from Wikipedia, which is also licensed CC BY-SA.

Changes: only the closed_qa category; rows chosen by a fixed hash of their text; passages
longer than a limit left out (the tiny variant 420 characters, the default 900); footnote markers
like [1] removed; each row's passage and question joined into one `prompt` column and the
person's answer kept as `answer`. scripts/make_dolly_example.py rebuilds the files.
